The name "Varyk" and the Varyk logo (the two-gear mark) are trademarks of
Vladislav Mickevic. The code in this repository is licensed under MIT or
Apache-2.0, and those licenses cover the code only. They do not grant any
right to use the name or the logo. This policy says how the name and the logo
may be used. Its purpose is to make sure that anything called Varyk is Varyk,
so that people are not misled about what they are getting or who made it.

The logo's files, and the rules for placing them, are in the
[`docs/brand`](https://github.com/Varyk-Lang/varyk.com/tree/main/docs/brand)
folder of the varyk.com repository.

## Allowed without asking

- Referring to the language and the project accurately: writing about Varyk,
  teaching it, comparing it with other languages, or saying that your software
  is written in Varyk or works with Varyk, with the name or the unmodified
  logo.
- Naming a library, tool, or plugin in a way that shows it is for Varyk rather
  than part of it, for example `serde-varyk` or "Foo for Varyk", as long as it
  does not suggest endorsement by the project and does not use the logo as its
  own.
- Distributing unmodified builds of this repository under the name Varyk and
  with the logo, or builds with changes needed only for packaging, such as for
  a Linux distribution.
- Running a meetup, a chat community, or a conference talk about Varyk, and
  showing the unmodified logo there, as long as it does not suggest it is
  official.

## Needs permission

- Naming a fork or a modified version of the compiler or its libraries Varyk,
  or anything confusingly similar, or giving it the logo or a logo
  confusingly similar to it. A fork is welcome, but it must use a different
  name and a different logo.
- Using the name or the logo in a product name, a product's logo, a company
  name, or a domain name.
- Selling goods or services under the name or with the logo, or suggesting
  that the project endorses, sponsors, or certifies you.
