# Trademark policy

"Varyk" is a trademark of Vladislav Mickevic. The code in this repository is
licensed under MIT or Apache-2.0, and those licenses cover the code only. They
do not grant any right to use the name. This policy says how the name may be
used. Its purpose is to make sure that anything called Varyk is Varyk, so that
people are not misled about what they are getting or who made it.

## Allowed without asking

- Referring to the language and the project accurately: writing about Varyk,
  teaching it, comparing it with other languages, or saying that your software
  is written in Varyk or works with Varyk.
- Naming a library, tool, or plugin in a way that shows it is for Varyk rather
  than part of it, for example `serde-varyk` or "Foo for Varyk", as long as it
  does not suggest endorsement by the project.
- Distributing unmodified builds of this repository under the name Varyk, or
  builds with changes needed only for packaging, such as for a Linux
  distribution.
- Running a meetup, a chat community, or a conference talk about Varyk, as
  long as it does not suggest it is official.

## Needs permission

- Naming a fork or a modified version of the compiler or its libraries Varyk,
  or anything confusingly similar. A fork is welcome, but it must use a
  different name.
- Using the name in a product name, a company name, or a domain name.
- Selling goods or services under the name, or suggesting that the project
  endorses, sponsors, or certifies you.

## Asking

Write to hello@varyk.com. Requests made in good faith are usually granted.
